## Why the two real full-cycle attempts failed

GitHub **listings** are eventually consistent — first the label-filtered listing lagged (search index, minutes), then — after the client-side dialect fix — the **unfiltered open-issues listing itself lagged >30s behind a freshly CREATED issue**. Direct GETs (`/repos/{o}/{r}/issues/{n}`, the dependencies read) are immediately consistent. The FACTORY tolerates listing lag by design (the pool is re-derived every tick and self-heals); the SMOKE was the thing demanding instant listing visibility. That demand is now gone.

## What changed

### 4. `src/tracker/github-sim.ts` — pre-existing bug fix (required by the new offline exercise)
- The sim's `runner` did `return this.gh(...)` **without `await`** inside its `try` — async rejections bypassed the catch, so failures came out as raw `SimHttpError` WITHOUT a `stderr`, while the sim's own doc promises gh-shaped errors ("the adapter's tolerances read that stderr"). The adapter's `typeof stderr === "string"` tolerance guard then broke (e.g. `releaseClaim`'s ensure-label hit "label already exists" and threw `PartialWriteError`). Fixed with `return await this.gh(...)` / `return await this.git(...)` — the documented contract is restored and the adapter's tolerance paths are now exercised for real (previously they silently degraded to message-fallback matching).
